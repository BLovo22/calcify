Common Mistakes

❌ Paying only the minimum. A \,000 balance at 20% APR with minimum payments could take nearly 10 years and cost over \,000 total.

❌ Adding new charges while paying off debt. If you keep swiping the card, you are running on a treadmill. Stop new charges until the balance is zero.

❌ Ignoring balance transfer offers. A 0% APR balance transfer card can save you thousands — but watch out for the 3-5% transfer fee.

What is the fastest way to pay off credit card debt?▼
The avalanche method (pay highest APR card first) saves the most money mathematically. The snowball method (pay smallest balance first) builds psychological momentum. Both work if you stick with it. Also consider a 0% APR balance transfer card or a debt consolidation loan at a lower rate.
