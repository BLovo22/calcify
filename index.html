❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are Numbrly calculators really free? ▼

Yes. Every calculator on Numbrly is completely free. No paywalls, no premium tiers, no hidden fees. We may display ads to support the site, but the tools remain unrestricted.

Is my data private? ▼

Absolutely. All calculations run entirely in your browser. No financial data is ever sent to a server, stored, or shared with anyone. You do not even need to create an account.

How accurate are the results? ▼

Our calculators use standard financial formulas (compound interest, amortization, etc.) and produce mathematically accurate results. However, they are for educational and planning purposes only — not financial advice.

Do I need to sign up? ▼

No. Numbrly requires no registration, no email, and no account. Just open any tool and start calculating immediately.
