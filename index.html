04 / NOTES

Before you use the answer.

What the tools do, what stays in your browser, and where an estimate stops being advice.

Are Numbrly calculators really free? +

Yes. Every calculator is free to use, with no account, premium tier, or locked result. Advertising may support the site, but it does not restrict the tools.

What happens to the numbers I enter? +

Calculations run in your browser. Some tools can remember entries on your device with local storage. Like most websites, hosting and advertising services may still receive ordinary usage data; see the privacy policy for details.

How should I interpret a result? +

Each result is an estimate based on the inputs and assumptions shown. It is useful for planning and comparison, but taxes, lender rules, market returns, fees, and personal circumstances can change the real outcome.

Do I need to sign up? +

No. Numbrly requires no registration or email. Open a tool, change the assumptions, and compare the result immediately.
