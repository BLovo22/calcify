Why this starts with total cost

A dealer can make almost any vehicle appear to fit by extending the term or increasing the down payment. A durable budget works in the opposite direction: choose a maximum all-in monthly transportation amount, reserve money for non-loan costs, and let the remaining payment determine the price.

Insurance

Get a quote for the exact vehicle before buying. Premiums vary by model, driver, location, coverage, and lender requirements.

Maintenance

New cars still need tires, service, registration, and deductibles. Used and luxury vehicles may need a larger monthly reserve.

Loan term

Long terms reduce the payment but increase interest and negative-equity risk. Compare 48, 60, and 72 months before choosing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What percentage of take-home pay should go to a car?
Treat any percentage as a starting point, not a rule. A household with expensive housing, childcare, or debt may need a lower cap. Test the all-in amount in your actual budget.
Does the affordable price include tax and fees?
The headline result is the estimated selling price. The model separately includes sales tax and entered fees when solving for the maximum financed amount.
Should I use a trade-in value before paying off its loan?
No. Enter only net trade-in credit: the vehicle value minus any loan payoff and transaction adjustments. A negative trade balance should be handled separately rather than treated as a down payment.
