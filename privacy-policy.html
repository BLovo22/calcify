🔐 Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

Calculator inputs and results are processed in your browser and are not sent to a Numbrly application server. Some tools save calculator values in your browser's local storage so they can be restored on your next visit. Where a tool provides a clear or reset action, you can use it to remove that tool's saved values; you can always remove saved values through your browser's controls for clearing this site's data.

2. Cookies & Third-Party Services

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may receive technical and usage information, such as your IP address, browser or device information, page visits, cookies, and advertising identifiers. This is separate from the values you enter into Numbrly calculators.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits. For more information, see Google's Advertising Policies.

3. GitHub Pages Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information such as IP addresses for security and operational purposes. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

4. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us through the GitHub repository associated with this site.
