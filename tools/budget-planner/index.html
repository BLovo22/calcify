Frequently Asked Questions

What is a monthly budget planner?▼
A monthly budget planner helps you organize income, expenses, savings, and remaining cash flow so you can make better financial decisions.
Does Numbrly save my budget online?▼
No. Your budget data is saved only in your browser using localStorage. Numbrly does not upload or store your financial information.
How is the Budget Score calculated?▼
The Budget Score uses rule-based checks for savings rate, rent as a percentage of income, and total expenses as a percentage of income.
What does the 50/30/20 rule mean?▼
The 50/30/20 rule suggests using 50% of income for needs, 30% for wants, and 20% for savings or investments.
Can I export my budget report?▼
Yes. You can download a PNG report or export your budget categories to CSV directly from the Budget Planner.
