Common Mistakes

❌ Comparing mortgage to rent 1:1. Add property taxes, insurance, and maintenance. The real cost of owning is 30-50% higher than the mortgage alone.

❌ Assuming rents stay flat. Rents typically increase 2-4% per year. Over 10 years, \,000 rent could become \,600+.

❌ Ignoring transaction costs. Closing costs (2-5%) and agent commissions (5-6%) add up. If you might move within 3-5 years, renting wins.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it better to rent or buy?▼
If you plan to stay 5+ years, buying is usually better because you build equity and lock in housing costs. Under 3-5 years, renting is typically cheaper after accounting for closing costs and agent commissions.
How long until buying is cheaper?▼
In most U.S. cities, buying becomes cheaper than renting after 3-7 years once you account for closing costs and equity buildup. Use different time periods in the calculator to find your specific break-even point.
